01

The first 30 days.

A new roof does some surprising things in the first month. Most are normal. A few aren't. Here's the difference — so you don't worry about something routine, or ignore something that matters.

Normal · don't worry

Things that show up in the first month

  • A brown stain on a new shingle — it's protective film, washes off in the first hard rain
  • A tar smell on the first hot day — the sealing strips are activating in the sun
  • Stray nails in the yard for the first week — we'll come re-sweep, no charge
  • A handful of granules in the gutters after the first rain — manufacturing surplus, not failure
  • Settling sounds at night — the wood adjusting to the new weight balance
  • Minor sap or pollen marks — usually washes off, or we'll come out
Watch for · call us

Things that mean call your foreman

  • Any water stain on a ceiling or upper wall — call same day
  • Visible shingle that lifted, curled, or moved — call within a few days
  • Daylight visible from the attic — call same day
  • A leak around a vent, chimney, or skylight — call same day
  • Persistent musty smell in a closet or attic — investigate within a week
  • A nail you find more than two weeks out — call, we re-sweep free
02

Your warranty — what it actually covers.

Every NC Roofing Service install ships with two separate warranties. They cover different things. Both are registered — by us, on your behalf — in the first 30 days after install. Confirmation goes in your handoff packet.

MFG

Manufacturer warranty

25 – 50 years · the shingles

From GAF, CertainTeed, or Owens Corning depending on what's on your house. Covers the shingle materials themselves — defects, premature wear, granule loss.

  • Manufacturing defects in the shingles
  • Premature granule loss beyond normal aging
  • Material failures from defects, not weather
  • Prorated after 10 years (most manufacturers)
  • Transferable to the next homeowner (one transfer, in writing)
NCRS

Workmanship warranty

10 years · the install

From us. Covers the install itself — everything our crew did with their hands. Standard 10 years; longer (up to 25) on Master Elite jobs with the GAF Golden Pledge.

  • Installation defects of any of the 9 components
  • Flashing or vent failures from our install
  • Decking issues we missed during tear-off
  • Ventilation imbalance we should have caught
  • We come back free for any covered issue, no deductible
What no warranty covers — honest about the exclusions:
  • Acts of god — tornadoes, hail above warranty threshold, falling trees (that's insurance)
  • Neglect — uncleaned gutters, ice dams from poor attic insulation, mold from leaks ignored for months
  • Third-party modifications — solar panel installs, satellite dishes, antennas we didn't install
  • Damage from another contractor working on your roof after us
  • Normal end-of-life aging after the warranty period
03

The maintenance timeline.

Five milestones, ten years apart at most. Fifteen minutes a year, plus one real inspection at year ten. That's what gets a 25-year shingle to actually last 25 years.

Day 30
First month

Confirm everything settled correctly

Walk the perimeter once. Look up. Confirm no curling, no lifted shingles, no stains inside. Pollen and sap marks usually rinse off by now. Granules in the gutter should have tapered to nothing.

Anything off? Call your foreman directly — the number is in the handoff packet. We'll come out free for anything that's not weather-related.
Day 90
Three months

First seasonal check

You've now seen the roof through at least one weather event — thunderstorm, wind gust, maybe early hail. Walk the property. Look at the gutters and downspouts. Inside, peek into the attic with a flashlight, check for daylight or stains.

Year 1
Anniversary

15-minute annual check

The maintenance habit starts here. From the ground, walk all four sides. Look at the gutters, look at the ridge, look at the flashing around the chimney. Do the attic flashlight check. The whole thing takes a quarter of an hour.

Or: book our free annual checkup. We come out, drone the roof, walk you through the photos. Free for our install customers. Forever.
Year 5
Halfway in

First "is anything aging fast?" check

Five years in, granule loss should still be minimal. Shingles should look almost new. If you're seeing visible color change, edge curling, or a lot of grit in the gutters, that's worth a closer look — either by you or by us, free.

Year 10
The big checkup

Free comprehensive inspection — we come to you

Year ten is the threshold where most architectural shingles begin showing real aging. We do a full inspection: drone shots of every facet, ladder check of every penetration, attic inspection, ventilation balance test. You get a written report with photos and a 10-year forecast. Free for everyone we installed for.

Why we do this for free: the people who get a year-10 checkup tend to keep our number when they eventually need a replacement. It costs us a half-day, and it earns us your next roof. Self-interest in the open.

04

When to call us back.

Some things warrant a call. Most things don't. Here's the honest list — both sides — so you're not paying us to come out for things that resolve themselves, and not ignoring things that get worse.

Call us

Things worth a call any time

  • Interior water stain. Any age. Call same day. Photograph before we arrive.
  • Visible damage from a storm. Before you call insurance. We help document.
  • Lifted or missing shingle. Single shingle, easy fix. Don't let it become five.
  • Sagging or wave in the roofline. Structural. Call before it gets worse.
  • Accelerating granule loss. Year 3+, if the gutters are gritty after every rain.
  • Anything that wasn't there before. Trust the instinct. Free to ask.
No need to call

Things that resolve themselves

  • Brown film stain on a new shingle. First 30 days only. Washes off.
  • Slight tar smell on a hot day. First month. Sealing adhesive curing.
  • A few popped nails. Yearly checkup will catch them. Or call if you want.
  • Curling at the very edge after year 18. Normal end-of-life. Plan replacement.
  • Light pollen or sap discoloration. Hose it off or wait for rain.
  • A handful of granules after a heavy storm. Normal weather wear.
